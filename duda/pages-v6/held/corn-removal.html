N° 01

You don't have to live covered up.

For years, corns may have pushed you to hide your feet: closed shoes in summer, toes tucked out of photos, discomfort with every step.

Our minimally invasive, in-office procedures are designed to treat the cause of the corn, for toes that look smoother and feel better in your shoes.

N° 02

Treating the core, not just the surface

A corn is rarely just a skin problem. Usually it's the surface sign of a bone prominence or a bent toe pressing against your shoe. Shaving or trimming it helps only for a while, because the pressure is still there. So when it's needed, Dr. Wagner corrects what's underneath:

  • Hammertoe correction.Realigns a bent toe joint to take pressure off the top of the toe.
  • Mallet toe correction.Releases a bent joint near the tip of the toe.
  • Structural decompression.Reshapes the bone prominence under the corn, with no pins or screws, with the goal of keeping the joint flexible.

Correcting the cause is how we aim to keep the corn from coming back.

N° 03
