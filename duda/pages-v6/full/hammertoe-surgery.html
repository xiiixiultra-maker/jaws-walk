N° 01

Why so many people put it off

Curved, bent or overlapping toes, commonly called hammertoes, claw toes or mallet toes, can cause discomfort, chronic friction corns and difficulty wearing fitted shoes. Many patients delay treatment out of fear of rigid joint fusions, visible wires or long recoveries.

Our approach uses tiny incisions to realign and straighten the toes, with the goal of preserving natural joint flexibility and movement.

N° 02

Our modern technique

Our procedure works with the natural mechanics of your toes to bring them back into alignment.

  • Hardware-free: no pins, screws or wires.We do not insert exposed K-wires, pins or internal metal screws into the toes.
  • No joint fusion.We do not fuse the toe joints together. The joint stays intact, with the goal of keeping natural movement and flexibility in your toes after recovery.
  • Hidden incisions.Incisions are kept as small as possible and placed underneath or in between the toes, where surgical marks are harder to see.
  • Awake, in our office.Performed in our office-based surgical suite under local anesthesia, with no general anesthesia.
  • On your feet the same day.The technique is designed so you walk out of the office the same day wearing a specialized post-operative boot, without crutches or a rigid cast. Full recovery takes longer, and Dr. Wagner maps out your timeline.
  • Splinting and bandaging.Alignment is held for the first 4 to 6 weeks with specialized surgical bandaging and splinting, while the soft tissue and bone stabilize.

N° 03

Minimally invasive vs. traditional hammertoe surgery

Understanding the key differences between surgical techniques helps you make an informed decision for your foot health and your aesthetic goals.

Personalized care & surgical philosophy

Podiatric surgeons differ in their training, clinical philosophy and preferred techniques. Traditional joint fusion with external pins is a standard, widely used approach. Our practice focuses on hardware-free, tiny-incision procedures designed to keep the joint flexible, performed under local anesthesia. Every treatment plan is tailored to your anatomy and your lifestyle goals.
