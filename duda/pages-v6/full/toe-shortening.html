N° 01

Restoring natural proportions

Minimally invasive toe shortening surgery is designed to restore natural proportions, relieve chronic discomfort and help you regain confidence in any footwear or barefoot setting.

Whether you are dealing with friction in your shoes or hiding your feet at the beach, the goal is the same: toes in harmony with the rest of your foot, and comfort to match.

N° 02

Functional & visual benefits

Visual & aesthetic

  • Proportionate foot contour.Shortens overly long toes, with the goal of a balanced, harmonious alignment across all toes.
  • Discreet healing.Tiny incisions are designed to leave less visible scarring than a traditional surgical incision.
  • Expanded footwear choices.The goal: open-toed shoes, sandals or fitted footwear, worn comfortably and without feeling self-conscious.

Function & comfort

  • Pressure and pain relief.Designed to relieve the constant friction and crowding at the tip of the toe inside shoes.
  • Corns and calluses.Designed to reduce the focal pressure points that cause painful corns, calluses and skin irritation.
  • Joint stress.Helps reduce the long-term joint stress that can lead to secondary deformities like hammertoes.

N° 03

The technique: no pins, screws or fusion

Our toe shortening surgery prioritizes tissue preservation and natural joint function.

  • No pins, screws or plates.We do not insert internal metal hardware that would need to be removed later.
  • No joint fusion.We preserve your natural joint articulation, with the goal that your toe keeps its flexibility and normal motion after surgery.
  • No internal fixation.Small instruments let us perform keyhole structural corrections through tiny, hidden access points.

N° 04

Minimally invasive vs. traditional toe surgery

Proportional balancing

Toe shortening is both a functional correction and a structural art. We evaluate the entire architecture of your forefoot so the shortened toe lines up with the toes beside it, with the goal of a natural, elegant foot contour.
