N° 01

What foot feminization means

A feminine foot usually comes down to a few details: a narrow forefoot, slender toes that taper evenly, smooth contours and a graceful arch. Foot feminization is a plan built around those details. Dr. Wagner looks at your whole foot, then chooses the procedures that move it toward the shape you have in mind.

N° 02

An invisible weight

For many people, feet that feel too broad or too large are an invisible weight. They limit your shoe choices and quiet your self-expression.

You don't have to compromise on your silhouette or settle for shoes that don't fit your style. Patients from all walks of life travel to JAWS from across the country and abroad, and every plan is made for one person: you.

N° 03

The procedures behind a feminine foot

Foot feminization often combines several procedures, chosen for your feet at your consultation and planned together toward a delicate, slender, proportioned silhouette. Working through tiny incisions for most of these procedures, Dr. Wagner reshapes the foot with the goal of preserving its natural function.

  1. 01

    Minimally invasive toe shortening

    Long or uneven toes can make the whole foot look out of proportion. Through tiny incisions, and without pins, plates or screws, Dr. Wagner shortens and realigns the toes, aiming for an even, tapered profile for open-toe shoes.

    Toe shortening →
  2. 02

    Minimally invasive bunion & bunionette correction

    A bump at the base of the big toe (bunion) or the little toe (tailor's bunion) widens the front of the foot. Through millimeter-sized incisions, Dr. Wagner reshapes and realigns the bone underneath to narrow the forefoot's silhouette.

    Bunion surgery →
  3. 03

    The Skinny Toe Tuck™

    For toes that hold extra soft-tissue fullness. Dr. Wagner's signature debulking technique subtly reshapes and slims each toe, for a sleeker, more refined line from base to tip.

    Foot & toe tucks →
  4. 04

    The Skinny Foot Tuck™

    Our flagship narrowing procedure for extra soft-tissue volume along the inner and outer edges of the foot. Sculpting these contours is designed to give you a slimmer profile for delicate shoes.

    Foot & toe tucks →
  5. 05

    Arch & foot contouring

    Refines the contours of the midfoot and arch, smoothing bony prominences and soft-tissue bulk for a more graceful arch profile.

  6. 06

    Corn & lesion removal

    Friction over a toe joint can leave a raised corn. Dr. Wagner removes it and, when needed, treats the bone prominence underneath, for smoother-looking skin over the joint.

    Corn removal →
  7. 07

    Minimally invasive toe straightening

    For overlapping, hammer or mallet toes that rub in your shoes. Through tiny incisions, Dr. Wagner realigns the soft tissue and bone to straighten the toe and smooth its contour.

    Hammertoe & toe straightening →
